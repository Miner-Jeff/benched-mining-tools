about=Processing tools that modify a DEM to represent planned mining. "Generate Benched Open Pit" projects a benched pit downward from an outline at a rim elevation to a floor elevation. "Generate Benched Waste Rock Pile" builds a benched dump upward and inward from a maximum-footprint outline. Both work at 1 m resolution in a UTM CRS, report cut/fill volume, and can write contour lines (with an ELEVATION attribute) inside the footprint. Optional haul road design follows the Deswik open pit design guidelines. LICENSE: GPL-2.0-or-later. DISCLAIMER: provided without warranty and used entirely at your own risk; it is a planning aid, not engineered mine design, and all output must be reviewed by a qualified mining or geotechnical engineer.
